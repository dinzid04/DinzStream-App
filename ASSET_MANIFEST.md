# ASSET_MANIFEST.md — Willowbrook Cove (Cozy Fishing Life RPG)

All visual and audio production assets are locally bundled inside `app/src/main/assets/rpg/` for 100% offline playability.
Every sprite uses a cohesive warm 16-bit Lospec-inspired palette with colored outlines (`#241C22`), transparent backgrounds, and nearest-neighbor (`TextureFilter.Nearest`) rendering.
